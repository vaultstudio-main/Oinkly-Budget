Piggybank Financial

Your money, organised, on your Mac. No account to create, no sign-in, and nothing leaves your computer.

Get it on the Mac App Store

What it does

Every kind of account

Checking, savings, cash, credit cards, loans, mortgages, investments and assets, together in one place.

Bills that don't sneak up

A calendar of what is due, reminders before each bill, and one step to pay it so every account stays in step.

Budgets and goals

Set monthly budgets for your categories and goals for saving up or paying off debt.

Clear reports

Income against spending, where the money goes, what you owe and how investments are growing.

Receipts attached

Keep a photo or PDF bill with the transaction, or mark it as having none.

Reminders you set

Get a notification to review your budget, check bills or back up, and jump straight to the right page.

Private by design

Piggybank Financial keeps everything on your own Mac. There are no accounts, no servers, no advertising and no tracking. Backups are a single file you control. Read the privacy policy.

A closer look

Good to know

  • Made for the Mac, running macOS 15 or later.
  • No iPhone or iPad app and no sync between devices. Move your data between Macs with a backup file.
  • Fast with years of history. It is built to stay quick with very large numbers of transactions.
