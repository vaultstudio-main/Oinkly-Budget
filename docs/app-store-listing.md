## Description (max 4000)
Piggybank Financial keeps your whole financial picture in one place, and keeps it private. Everything you enter stays on your own Mac. There is no account to create, no sign-in, no ads and no tracking.

KEEP EVERY ACCOUNT TOGETHER
Checking, savings, cash, credit cards, loans, mortgages, investments and assets all live side by side. Each account has its own register with a running balance, and you can see everything together on one Transactions page.

NEVER MISS A BILL
A calendar shows what is due, what is paid and what is next. Get a reminder before each bill, with extra attention for the ones that matter most. Paying a bill is one step: it comes out of your account, comes off what you owe, ticks the bill off and files under the right category.

PLAN AND TRACK
Set monthly budgets for your categories, and goals for saving up or paying off debt. Reports show income against spending, where your money goes, what you owe and how your investments are growing, by month, quarter or year.

OLD SCHOOL ON PURPOSE
Piggybank Financial doesn't link to your bank or any online account, and it never asks for a login. You go through your own statements and bills and enter what happened. When you handle your own numbers, you know where you stand and what your budget really is. You can also import a CSV file you've downloaded yourself.

DETAILS THAT FEEL RIGHT
• Credit limits are real: a charge that would reach a card's limit is stopped before it's saved
• Cash on Hand fills from ATM withdrawals, so the money in your wallet stays honest
• Attach a receipt photo or the PDF bill to any transaction
• Double-click a transaction for a closer look in its own window
• Dates, categories and amounts change in one deliberate place, never by a stray click
• A menu bar item shows your upcoming bills at a glance
• Your own reminders: review the budget each month, check bills each week, or back up
• Import transactions from a CSV file, and export when you like

FAST, EVEN WITH YEARS OF HISTORY
Piggybank is built to stay quick with very large numbers of transactions, so it feels the same in year five as in week one.

YOURS TO KEEP
Backups are a single file you control. Save everything, restore it on a new Mac, or keep a copy somewhere safe. There is no subscription and no data held hostage.

Piggybank Financial is made for the Mac and requires macOS 15 or later. It does not sync with other devices.

## Notes for the reviewer (App Review Information)
No sign-in is needed and there is no demo account. On first launch, the app opens an account setup screen: add a checking
account, then use File ▸ New Transaction to add a few transactions. Bills are on the Bills page and Reports show the result.
Notifications are optional and local; the app asks the first time a bill reminder is scheduled. The app makes no network
connections and collects no data.
