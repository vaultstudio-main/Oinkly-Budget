Oinkly Budget

Personal finance that stays on your Mac. Track every account, never miss a bill, plan with budgets and goals, and see where your money goes, without handing your data to anyone.

  • Checking, savings, cards, loans, mortgages and investments in one place
  • Bills calendar with reminders you control

Old school on purpose

Oinkly Budget doesn't connect to your bank or any online account. There is no login and no link to a bank. You go through your own statements and bills, so you always know where you stand and what your budget really is.

How I build

Private first

Your information stays on your device. There is nothing to leak because nothing is collected.

Made for the Mac

Native apps that feel at home: menu bar items, notifications, keyboard shortcuts and a proper Help.

Fast and calm

Honest and simple
