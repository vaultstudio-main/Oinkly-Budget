Oinkly Budget

Your money, organised, on your Mac. No account to create, no sign-in, and nothing leaves your computer.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store

$9.99 · one-time purchase · no subscription

What it does

Every kind of account

Checking, savings, cash, credit cards, loans, mortgages, investments and assets, together in one place.

Bills that don't sneak up

A calendar of what is due, bills to tick off, reminders before each one, and a single step to pay it so every account stays in step.

Repeating transactions

Set up your paycheque, rent or anything that repeats. Each date adds an entry for you to confirm, and shows in the calendar, the menu bar and your reminders.

Splits and tags

Put one payment across several categories, and label transactions with your own colored tags.

Budgets and goals

Set monthly budgets for your categories, and goals that follow your accounts so progress updates itself.

Clear reports

Income against spending, where the money goes, what you owe and how investments are growing, with six-month projections.

Receipts attached

Keep a photo or PDF bill with the transaction, or mark it as having none.

Menu bar and widget

See what is due this month at a glance: bills, repeating transactions and your reminders in one list, names and dates only.

Import and export

Bring in a CSV from your bank with a preview and Undo, and save statements as PDF or CSV.

Reminders you set

Get a notification to review your budget, check bills or back up, and jump straight to the right page.

Old school on purpose

Oinkly Budget doesn't link to your bank, your cards or any online account, and it never asks for a login.

You go through your own statements and bills and enter what happened. That's deliberate. When you handle your own numbers, you know exactly where you stand, and you know what your budget really is, instead of watching a feed you didn't touch.

Have a file from your bank? You can import a CSV you've downloaded yourself, with a preview first and Undo afterwards. Nothing is ever connected.

Private by design

Oinkly Budget keeps everything on your own Mac. There are no accounts, no servers, no advertising and no tracking. Reminders and the widget never show amounts. Backups are a single file you control, and a copy of your data is made before an upgrade. Read the privacy policy.

Good to know

  • No bank linking. You enter your own transactions, or import a CSV file you downloaded yourself.
  • Made for the Mac, running macOS 15 or later (Apple silicon and Intel).
  • No iPhone or iPad app and no sync between devices. Move your data between computers with a backup file.
  • Always a way out: a backup is one file, and nothing is held hostage.
  • Fast with years of history. It is built to stay quick with very large numbers of transactions.
